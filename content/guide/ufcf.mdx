## Worked example

| Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EBIT | 165.00 | 181.50 | 199.65 | 219.62 | 241.58 |
| NOPAT (× (1 − 25% tax)) | 123.75 | 136.13 | 149.74 | 164.71 | 181.18 |
| + D&A | 55.00 | 60.50 | 66.55 | 73.21 | 80.53 |
| − Capex (6% of revenue) | 66.00 | 72.60 | 79.86 | 87.85 | 96.63 |
| − Δ NWC (10% of revenue increase) | 10.00 | 11.00 | 12.10 | 13.31 | 14.64 |
| **UFCF** | **102.75** | **113.03** | **124.33** | **136.76** | **150.44** |

```
nopat[t]  = ebit[t] × (1 - taxRate)
ufcf[t]   = nopat[t] + da[t] - capex[t] - nwcChange[t]
```

This matches `projectUnleveredFreeCashFlow` in
[`/lib/dcf.ts`](https://github.com/rishitjain2609/dcf-lab/blob/main/lib/dcf.ts) — the same function
the [game](/game) uses once it's live.
