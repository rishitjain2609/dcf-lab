## The running example's assumptions

Carried over from [Setup & conventions](/guide/setup-and-conventions):

| Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue growth | 12% | 11% | 10% | 9% | 8% |
| EBITDA margin | 22% | 22% | 22% | 22% | 22% |
| D&A, % of revenue | 4% | 4% | 4% | 4% | 4% |
| Capex, % of revenue | 5% | 5% | 5% | 5% | 5% |
| Δ NWC, % of revenue increase | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% |
| Tax rate | 25% | 25% | 25% | 25% | 25% |

Growth fades from 12% toward a more sustainable 8% by Year 5 — margins and capex/NWC intensity are
held flat here for simplicity; a real model would flex these too.
