## Worked example

| Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue (₹ cr) | 1,100.00 | 1,210.00 | 1,331.00 | 1,464.10 | 1,610.51 |
| EBITDA (20% margin) | 220.00 | 242.00 | 266.20 | 292.82 | 322.10 |
| D&A (5% of revenue) | 55.00 | 60.50 | 66.55 | 73.21 | 80.53 |
| EBIT | 165.00 | 181.50 | 199.65 | 219.62 | 241.58 |

```
ebitda[t] = revenue[t] × 20%
ebit[t]   = ebitda[t] - da[t]
```
