## Worked example

| Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue (₹ cr) | 1,120.00 | 1,243.20 | 1,367.52 | 1,490.60 | 1,609.84 |
| EBITDA (22% margin) | 246.40 | 273.50 | 300.85 | 327.93 | 354.17 |
| D&A (4% of revenue) | 44.80 | 49.73 | 54.70 | 59.62 | 64.39 |
| EBIT | 201.60 | 223.78 | 246.15 | 268.31 | 289.77 |

```
ebitda[t] = revenue[t] × ebitdaMargin
ebit[t]   = ebitda[t] - da[t]
```
