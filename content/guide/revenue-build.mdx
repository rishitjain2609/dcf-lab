## Worked example

Starting from ₹1,000 cr and the growth rates from [Assumptions](/guide/assumptions):

| Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue (₹ cr) | 1,120.00 | 1,243.20 | 1,367.52 | 1,490.60 | 1,609.84 |

```
revenue[t] = revenue[t-1] × (1 + growth[t])
1,120.00 = 1,000.00 × 1.12
1,243.20 = 1,120.00 × 1.11
```

Computed by `projectUnleveredFreeCashFlow` in [`/lib/dcf.ts`](/guide/ufcf).
